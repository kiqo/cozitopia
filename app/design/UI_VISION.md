# Cozitopia UI vision

Warm, flat, hand-drawn-feel home: cream paper, coral and sage, serif headlines, soft shapes.

- Every screen opens with one illustrated banner.
- Each screen has one primary action and one small reward (a wobble, a sticker or a window light).
- Tapping a banner wobbles its scene.
- Banners show the whole scene on wide screens; the house is never cropped out.
